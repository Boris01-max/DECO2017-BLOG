# Interpreting the brief

The brief have made itself clear. we are going to bulid the platform whith is meant to encourage particitation, communication, and connection between people with common interests, instead of users passively consuming content.

I noticed that the term "information" and "experience" are mentioned several times as the core of the platform. It is prompt that tells us this project is focus on operating system rather than visual design alone.

One more interesting thing is that the brief does not define a specific type of community, which creates opportunities to explore different idea and idnetities. It may test how behavious, motivations, and needs full work on the creative community we bulid.

From my perspective, the biggest challenge of this project will be balancing simplicity and engagement. Many social platform today are overcrowded with information and features, the successfull online community hub should feel focused, intuitive, and meanful. User should immefiately understand the purpose of the platform and feel naturally encouraged to inetracat with others.

# Project diraction

During ideation stage, we need to set and understand what type of community we are going to build at first. Ideally, the topic we choose should notivate us and also we are interesting in. It will help us stay passion and come up better inspiratiion. 

After discussion, there is a word left on the table.
                              "Cemeteries"                                  
this idea is from my teammate. I reckon that she has some kind of experience or need, because I found passion on her eyes. It will help us go deeper on identifying core functional requirements. 

One thing that makes this direction interesting is that cemeteries are usually associated with death, silence, and memories, but we started thinking about them as a form of digital connection instead. Rather than only focusing on sadness, the platform could become a place where people preserve stories, memories, and emotional attachment. This immediately separates the idea from traditional online communities because the interaction is slower, more emotional, and more reflective.

As we continued discussing the concept, we started imagining how digital interaction could work inside this type of community. For example, users may create memorial spaces, upload stories, share memories, leave flowers, or interact with others who experienced similar loss. Instead of chasing attention through endless scrolling content, the platform could encourage meaningful participation and emotional support between users.

I also think this direction has strong potential visually and conceptually. The atmosphere, interaction style, and emotional tone would feel very different from mainstream social platforms. This gives us opportunities to experiment with immersive design, symbolism, and storytelling while still focusing on accessibility and usability.
