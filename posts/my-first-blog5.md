# Revisiting earlier assumptions

At the beginning of the project, we assumed stronger community interaction would naturally improve user engagement and emotional connection. However, as the platform structure became clearer, we realised memorial interaction behaves very differently from traditional online communities.

One important thing we discovered was that emotional reflection sometimes benefits from slower pacing, reduced interaction, and even silence within the interface. Features that normally increase activity in social platforms often weakened the memorial atmosphere of the project. This changed how we approached interaction design and helped us focus more on emotional pacing and quieter user experiences instead of maximising engagement.

# Accessibility considerations

As the project developed further, accessibility became more important during evaluation because the platform focuses heavily on reading stories and emotional reflection rather than quick interaction.

One issue we identified was that memorial pages could become difficult to read when large amounts of text, images, and interaction buttons appeared together on the same screen. Earlier layouts placed interactions such as flowers, comments, and memorial content too closely together, making the visual hierarchy unclear. Because of this, we started separating interaction areas from the main memorial stories and increasing spacing between content sections.

Another issue involved long-form memorial writing. Earlier concepts allowed unrestricted story length, but we realised large text blocks could reduce readability during extended browsing. Because of this, we started considering shorter memory sections and clearer content separation.

We also found that some interaction buttons were too hidden because we originally tried to make the interface feel minimal and emotional. In several layouts, actions such as leaving flowers, opening memories, or returning to previous pages were not immediately clear. This may create accessibility problems for users unfamiliar with the platform or older users who rely on clearer navigation cues. Because of this, we started making important buttons larger, separating them more clearly from decorative elements, and keeping important interactions in more consistent positions across pages.

# Interaction evaluation

One important question during evaluation was whether lightweight interactions such as flowers and short text responses were emotionally meaningful enough for users. Because the platform intentionally avoids large discussion systems, these smaller interactions became responsible for most user participation inside memorial pages.

One issue we identified was that some interactions may feel too minimal or emotionally unclear when placed directly beside long memorial stories. During reflection, we realised users may not immediately understand the purpose of temporary grave posts or why certain interactions disappear over time. This made interaction clarity more important than interaction quantity.

We also noticed that placing interaction systems too close to memorial stories sometimes interrupted the reading flow of emotional content. Because of this, we started separating memorial reading areas from interaction areas more clearly so users could first focus on memories before deciding whether to interact.
